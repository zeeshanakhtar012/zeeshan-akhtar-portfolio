Design & Engineering
How I build
products that last

Every decision — from architecture to animation — serves a single goal: ship something that works at scale without rewrites.

01
Architecture-first development
Clean Architecture with clear separation between data, domain, and presentation. Code that survives team changes and feature growth.
02
Async-first delivery
Weekly structured updates via tracking logs and video demos. No meetings for the sake of status — your time is your product.
03
Platform-native thinking
I design for Android and iOS idioms — not lowest-common-denominator cross-platform. Each platform gets what it deserves.
04
Scale-aware from day one
Offline-first, optimistic UI, proper error boundaries. Apps that work in London and rural Pakistan with equal reliability.
